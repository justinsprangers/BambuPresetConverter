Bambu Preset Converter

Copy your Bambu Studio filament and process presets to other Bambu printers. Runs offline; nothing is uploaded.

Nozzle size is never changed. A 0.4 mm preset becomes a 0.4 mm preset on the new printer, and is locked to that size. If the target has no matching nozzle or Bambu base preset, that copy is skipped. Your originals are not touched.
Drop .3mf projects, preset .json files, .bbsflmt bundles, or a whole folder here
To load all your saved presets, choose %APPDATA%\BambuStudio\user (paste that into the folder picker's address bar).
